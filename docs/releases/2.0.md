# Sauce Tracker 2.0 — two sources, one complete app

2.0 is a generation change, not simply 1.9 with another provider added. Sauce
Tracker now brings **NHentai and MangaDex into the same library, browser, reader,
profiles and local data model**, and brings that experience to a completely rebuilt
Desktop Bridge. It reuses the interactions and polish of the existing app instead
of creating a second, disconnected MangaDex interface.

The scale of this release is as much about connecting everything as adding new
screens: an imported manga can participate in the same search, tags, artists,
history, trends, heatmaps, recommendations, subscriptions and backups as a gallery.
Your phone remains the owner of your collection. No account or cloud service is
required.

## Start here: upgrading and default profiles

- Install the signed `Sauce-Tracker-2.0-release.apk` over the official 1.9 release;
  **do not uninstall 1.9 first**. Android 8.0/API 26 remains supported.
- New installs and 1.9 upgrades start with two source-locked profiles: **NHentai**
  and **MangaDex**. NHentai opens first after migration, with existing 1.9 data.
- Existing library entries, ratings, read/pin state, tags, creators, reading history,
  subscriptions and their events are migrated, not replaced by a fresh library.
- Old V1 backups import into NHentai even if MangaDex is currently active.
- Debug and release are separate installations. Debug profiles are not silently
  transferred into the release app.
- Make a fresh export before upgrading. See the [migration and recovery guide](https://github.com/Roinur/Sauce-tracker/blob/v2.0.0/docs/SAUCE_TRACKER_2.0_MIGRATION.md).
- V2 exports are designed for 2.0; **do not expect 1.9 to restore a 2.0 backup**.

## First-class MangaDex, without reinventing the app

- Both providers use a versioned source-adapter layer for search, browsing, detail,
  import, metadata refresh, creators and capability-aware actions.
- Entries have `(source, remote ID)` identities. A MangaDex UUID cannot collide
  with an NHentai gallery number.
- MangaDex uses the existing Entries cards, thumbnails and Selected Entry details,
  including the familiar Details disclosure—not a separate “profile library”.
- Covers, volume covers, alternate titles, authors/artists, tags, languages and
  chapter metadata are recognized by the shared systems.
- Long MangaDex UUIDs are kept out of everyday presentation; Copy ID remains
  available. NHentai's familiar numeric codes remain visible where useful.
- One Search Everything field understands known URLs/IDs and translates structured
  tag, creator and language searches to the appropriate provider.
- In a mixed profile, known input selects its provider; ambiguous Browser input
  uses the app's source-choice popup rather than opening the wrong site.
- Explicit tag mappings connect equivalent metadata across providers. Unknown or
  provider-specific tags remain intact rather than being fuzzy-merged.
- MangaDex Browser supports staged SFW, Suggestive and NSFW scope selection through
  a long press on its title.
- Browse, search, import/update and creators stay inside Sauce Tracker. Provider
  errors are isolated rather than taking down the other provider or local library.
- NHentai retains its established browsing, duplicate checks, reader and downloads.

## Profiles that separate personal state, not duplicate everything

- Create source-locked profiles or your own combined profiles with both sources.
- Shared remote metadata is stored once; read state, ratings, pins, local tags,
  history, subscriptions, training and recommendation state are profile-specific.
- Use the same remote entry in two profiles with different personal state.
- Quickly switch profiles by holding the Settings gear; profile management is in
  Settings rather than taking over the dashboard front.
- Profile create/edit/manage/transfer views use the app's rounded surfaces, custom
  popup language and privacy behavior.
- Copy or move entries between profiles, with Search Everything and tag filters
  making selection practical in larger libraries.
- Transfers preserve resume positions, chapter progress and sessions. Existing
  destination state is merged safely; moves remove the source membership only
  after the destination copy succeeds.
- Mixed profiles expose pinned **Source** tags at the top of Tags, separated by a
  small divider. Single-source profiles do not show redundant source tags.
- Equivalent mixed-source subscriptions are not counted twice in the displayed
  totals; source/profile routing remains distinct behind the scenes.

## Manga chapters inside the familiar slideshow

- Compact chapter lists in Library and Browser, with volume-cover thumbnails when
  available, replace an impractically large whole-series page overview.
- Flip chapter presentation between oldest-first and newest-first.
- Library initially centers the saved resume chapter in either order; ordinary
  manual scrolling does not keep snapping back.
- A new manga's large cover opens its earliest readable chapter. Once you have
  started reading, it resumes your saved chapter and exact page.
- Selecting a partly read chapter resumes its saved page instead of starting over.
- Continue from the last page of one chapter into the next, or go backward into the
  preceding chapter, using the same horizontal/vertical reader experience.
- Chapter-boundary page indicators identify the next chapter instead of presenting
  every chapter boundary as a gallery exit.
- Read-progress shimmer fills only the completed portion of each chapter row;
  thumbnails and text stay above the highlight layer.
- MangaDex series ratings are manual through the entry stars. Closing a chapter
  does not repeatedly ask you to rate the entire manga.
- NHentai retains its Done/reread rating flow; MangaDex Browser Done closes directly.
- Language chips are compact and evenly spaced. Import asks for a language unless
  one has already been selected.
- Different imported languages of the same series are separate entries, with
  language metadata in Library—not one combined progress record for every language.
- Chapter counts, chapter metadata and volume-cover caching are local metadata,
  **not a full chapter download**. Streaming and temporary evictable caches remain
  distinct from explicitly downloaded galleries.

## Raw-page capture and your saved screenshots

- Where Android exposes screenshot detection, the reader can offer the original,
  maximum-quality page instead of relying only on a display-resolution screenshot.
- The existing ordinary screenshot is not silently blocked or deleted.
- The hold-anywhere direction chooser also has a compact accent-colored screenshot
  target. Hover and release to save the raw page without changing reader mode.
- Its pickup area is circular and bounded, not the entire area below the chooser.
- Captures go into the existing Experimental Gallery with entry/source/language
  association; identifiable older captures can be matched without rewriting files.
- **Saved: Screenshots** in Library opens only that entry's saved captures.
  The remote Browser does not show this local-files link.
- Capture and gallery navigation keep the existing privacy/app-lock safeguards.

## History, intelligence and discovery now understand both sources

Reading Trends, Train your model, Tag Presets, Library Health and Verified Restore
already existed in 1.9. Their **multi-source/profile integration** is new in 2.0.

- MangaDex page visits, chapters, elapsed reading time and resume/progress records
  feed the shared data paths, rather than counting only a completed whole series.
- Chapter completion is recorded once; repeatedly crossing a chapter boundary does
  not repeatedly count the entire chapter as read.
- MangaDex History groups by local day and series: chapter/page/time totals in a
  meaningful card instead of many one-page session rows.
- Trends and heatmaps use the active profile/source scope. Chapters and pages keep
  their real units instead of being mislabeled as interchangeable.
- Large NHentai collections no longer freeze Heatmap Overview while matching tag
  catalogs: matching is indexed and preparation runs off the UI thread.
- MangaDex recommendations use the existing taste scorer, category weights,
  explicit training, presets, blocked tags and hidden/skipped state.
- Suggestions preserve provider identity through opening/import and do not silently
  switch a MangaDex-only profile back to NHentai.
- Mixed-source recommendation results are provider-balanced; established fallback
  behavior remains so small weight changes do not trivially empty the results.
- Tags and creators participate in counts, filtering, heatmaps and trends. Author
  subscriptions preserve punctuation, including names such as `TYPE-MOON`.
- Source-aware subscriptions, inbox events and notification routes belong to the
  right profile/provider; bell state refreshes after import/restore.
- Random and Suggested previews navigate and scroll to the actual selected entry.
- Sauce Finder builds its index from **downloaded entries first, then read entries,
  then the rest**, while remaining incremental and resumable.
- Sauce Finder includes MangaDex covers; it does not claim a downloaded full-series
  page index for MangaDex.
- Removed the local 1,000-entry truncation. Database paging and API concurrency
  remain bounded; removing a local cap does not remove rate-limit safeguards.

## Desktop Bridge 2.0: stay inside Sauce Tracker

- Completely rebuilt as maintainable static web assets served directly by the
  Android app, with a modern sidebar, dashboard, library and Selected Entry layout.
- Uses the app artwork and accent-aware icons, not placeholder diamonds or a
  separate desktop visual identity.
- Profile-aware dashboard counts, library shortcuts and recent reading.
- Search Everything, filters, sorting and bounded pagination; filters apply before
  pagination, and Load more is shown only when another page exists.
- List/grid layouts, readable cards, minimal tags in expanded details, and a
  workspace that uses its full width when no Selected Entry pane is needed.
- Internal NHentai/MangaDex Browser, imports/updates, entry details, tags and
  Artists / Groups. No duplicate standalone Groups section.
- Internal horizontal/vertical slideshow: read without leaving for an external
  provider tab, so reading progress and time can be tracked by Sauce Tracker.
- Fit-to-page horizontal viewing, circular centered navigation, wheel paging,
  restrained reader chrome, vertical zoom and an in-reader chapter drawer.
- Adjacent-page prefetch, profile-owned resume, page progress and chapter completion;
  session checkpoints use stable identities and idle-aware reading time.
- NHentai Done opens its rating flow; MangaDex uses manual series-level ratings.
- Grouped History, statistics/Reading Trends, phone-backed recommendations and
  on-demand Tag/Entry Heatmaps, including frequency views.
- Read/rating/pin/import actions use source identity and explicit profile ownership.
- Theme/accent controls sync with the phone. AUTO uses the phone's resolved Material
  You wallpaper palette—even when no manual accent is selected.
- Works in a normal browser tab and as a browser-provided app/shortcut window where
  Chrome/Edge support it. The phone must remain on the same local network.
- Clear certificate, challenge, current URL and changed-IP guidance. There is no
  separate Windows executable, cloud server, relay, service worker or offline PWA.
- Installation-specific TLS credentials, unlock challenge, rate limiting and
  compatibility handling remain in place. Incognito stops Bridge.
- Private API/image responses use `no-store`; private DOM state is cleared on
  background/disconnection. Stale/canceled responses cannot refill private views.
- Prefetch caches are bounded in-memory caches, not permanent browser-library
  storage; polling is bounded and paused when hidden.

## QR Share: small packages, not backups

- Share **one to ten** entries from either provider in a compact, versioned,
  compressed and checksummed QR payload.
- Searchable selection, QR image generation and Android sharing/saving.
- Camera and saved-image scan paths, useful for receiving a shared QR without a
  second physical screen.
- Preview source/title/identity and optional shared read/rating/pin/local tags
  before approving import. Remote metadata comes through the provider adapter.
- Existing personal status is not overwritten by default; applying shared status
  is explicit. Local tags merge rather than being deleted.
- Unknown sources, corrupt checksums, duplicate identities and oversized payloads
  are checked before import; individual provider failures produce partial results.
- Sessions, reading time, training, taste weights, subscriptions, media and security
  information are excluded. Text/files remain the format for larger batches.
- QR uses the existing Experimental Gallery design language, app accents and
  rounded surfaces; the camera FileProvider startup crash is fixed.
- Shared status/tags can be personal. A checksum detects corruption, not sender
  authenticity.

## Faster, bounded and more resilient

- Reused the shared connection pool and adapters across Browser, Library, Reader
  and Bridge instead of duplicating networking for each surface.
- MangaDex Browser reuses the established persistent, bounded thumbnail pipeline,
  smaller list covers, cached first paint and coalesced in-flight requests.
- Parallel independent feed requests; cached chapter metadata/counts/volume covers
  avoid refetching an entire series just to redisplay its list.
- Exact selected-chapter/saved-page warming, evictable reader manifests and removal
  of redundant preflight image probes reduce launch work.
- Prioritized visible-page decode followed by bounded forward page read-ahead.
  Adjacent chapters do not compete with the selected page during first paint.
- Stable MangaDex image-host routing with fallback, visible loading state until a
  bitmap exists, streaming decode and byte-bounded image caches.
- Temporary reader HTTP caching is evictable and separate from downloads.
- Memory-pressure handling releases reader/browser/dashboard bitmap caches;
  downsampled thumbnails and bounded startup concurrency reduce heap exhaustion.
- Retry/error handling isolates provider failures, retains local data and preserves
  cancellation rather than allowing older requests to overwrite new views.

## Appearance and interaction polish

- Stronger accent-aware artwork for Entries, Tags, Artists / Groups, Subscriptions,
  Heatmap and History, also reused inside their pages and Desktop Bridge.
- Rounded tag artwork, three-person Artists icon and a simple clock for History.
- Material-style vector pin instead of the realistic pin emoji in swipe gestures;
  clearer contrast for Unpin/Unread feedback and preserved icon detail across accents.
- Opt-in **Extra dark** under Settings / Personalization, using Book Tracker's dark
  surface treatment without replacing light/system modes or the chosen accent.
- Consistent rounded loading indicators, clipped press feedback, compact language
  spacing and profile/source choice popups that use the existing app components.
- Modern Dashboard work does not replace the intentionally retained Legacy Home.

## Safer upgrades, backups and diagnostics

- Explicit SQLite migration, a validated pre-migration snapshot, transactional
  rollback and integrity checks; no destructive “reset to an empty database” fallback.
- Snapshot validation compares preserved schemas/tables/rows/content before reuse;
  stale snapshots are retained rather than trusted or silently overwritten.
- Migrated NHentai identities, read/rating/pin/dates, tags, creators, sessions and
  subscriptions are validated before an upgrade commits.
- Fixed the real first-launch constructor-order crash found during a 1.9 upgrade.
- V2 exports include sources, profiles, memberships/state, profile preferences,
  history, subscriptions, MangaDex resume/progress and cached chapter/volume metadata.
- V1 remains supported. Profile-selective V2 restore does not clear another active
  profile's history/subscriptions.
- Preflight rejects duplicate identities, broken owners, malformed title JSON,
  invalid progress and incomplete multi-source payloads before writes.
- Proper alternate-title serialization preserves quotes, backslashes and multiline
  titles; malformed earlier development payloads are repaired safely.
- Current/procedural snapshots and shared thumbnail archives retain established
  manual-export protection; diagnostics do not create folders or rotate backups.
- Verified Restore restores into isolation twice, checks idempotence and strict
  source-platform/history/subscription/settings comparisons, and reports MangaDex
  entry/session/resume/chapter-progress counts explicitly.
- Legitimate browser-only or removed-entry sessions appear as **Reading outside
  library**, not a permanently unhealthy backup. They are retained; invalid owners
  and restore mismatches still fail. Older rows do not invent missing provenance.
- Privacy-safe GitHub Media Mode copies into a separate, dependency-ordered test
  database; reviewing screens and test profiles does not mutate the real library.

## Scope and verification

- Official providers: NHentai and MangaDex only. The adapter design is extensible,
  but other providers are not promised.
- MangaDex full offline chapter downloads remain hidden. Metadata/temporary caches
  are not presented as completed downloads.
- Version Museum is **deferred beyond 2.0**; its implementation is retained but its
  app entry and packaging are disabled. Custom accent wheels, accounts/cloud sync,
  remote Bridge and a separate desktop binary are not part of this release.
- Regression coverage includes source identity, QR limits, profile transfer,
  interrupted migration/rollback/retry, backup integrity and Bridge reader/privacy
  behavior. Real-device 1.9 upgrade and populated MangaDex Verified Restore have
  also been exercised. Full evidence and remaining manual coverage are kept in
  the [release checklist](https://github.com/Roinur/Sauce-tracker/blob/v2.0.0/TEST_CHECKLIST_2.0.md); automated fixtures are not
  described as proof of every device/provider/browser combination.
